Method

How the rankings were built

Pressure

A win-probability model built from the data pack scores every ball by how far one ordinary delivery could swing the match. The tensest 25% of balls in each innings, plus balls during a batting collapse, count as pressure.

Performance

Batters are valued by how much each ball changed their side's chance of winning. Bowlers are valued by runs saved against the average for the same situation.

Fair comparison

Short records are pulled towards average, IPL counts more than bilateral T20Is, recent seasons count more, and only players with an above-average record on pressure balls are ranked.

Reliability

Matches were resampled 500 times; the top-10 rate is how often a player stays in. The rankings were also rerun under alternative pressure definitions and sample sizes.

Rankings use only the Round 1 data pack (IPL and T20I, 2022-26). The auction lists add the official IPL 2027 retained lists published on 17 November 2026. Full method, tests and limitations are in the submitted report.
